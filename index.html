1
ACCOUNTABLE PARTNER

Headquartered in Seoul, Fareast Development connects a network of leading designers, stylists and master makers across Asia's design capitals. For each commission we curate a dedicated team around the project's ambition — hospitality, residential or commercial — pairing international creative direction with deep production capability across Korea, China and Vietnam.

We design and style — we do not build. Working alongside architects, developers and operators on both sides of the Pacific, we take responsibility for how a space feels: its concept, its interiors, its furniture and the objects that give it life.
